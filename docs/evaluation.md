# Evaluation & Auditing Methodology

> Status: Pending Implementation — Early Development

## 1. Overview

Documentation detailing the methodology, scoring formulas, and benchmarks used by TasteAudit AI to evaluate cultural recommendations.

## 2. Planned Audit Metrics

- **Cultural Coherence:** Measures alignment across cross-domain recommendations based on Qloo taste affinity data.
- **Affinity Distribution:** Evaluates depth of connection between paired entities and targeted demographics.
- **Hallucination & Anomaly Detection:** Identifies unsupported or non-correlated cultural claims.
